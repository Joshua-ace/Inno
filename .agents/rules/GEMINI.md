# Inno — Project Rules

These rules are non-negotiable and apply to every task in this project.

---

## Design Prohibitions (Never Do These)

- **No purple gradients** — avoid purple-dominant or purple-to-X gradient color schemes entirely.
- **No pill-shaped buttons** — use squared or subtly rounded corners (border-radius max ~6px) on CTAs and buttons.
- **No fake reviews or fake metrics** — do not fabricate testimonials, star ratings, user counts, revenue figures, or any social proof that is not real.
- **No vague hero text** — headlines must be specific, concrete, and meaningful. No "The future of X" or "Empowering Y" filler copy.
- **No emoji icons** — do not use emoji as UI icons or decorative elements. Use a proper icon library (e.g., Lucide, Heroicons) or SVG.
- **No em dashes** — use alternative punctuation (commas, colons, parentheses, or restructure the sentence).
- **No excessive scroll animations** — minimal, purposeful motion only. No parallax-heavy sections, no element-after-element entrance cascades.
- **No AI slop images** — do not use low-quality AI-generated stock imagery. Every image must be intentional and high quality.
- **No AI copy** — all written content must be deliberate and human-quality. No filler paragraphs, no generic benefit lists.
- **No cursor animations** — do not add custom cursor effects or cursor-following animations.
- **No fake counters** — do not use animated number counters that display unverifiable statistics.

- [ ] Connected to a custom domain.
- [ ] Site favicon is set (not the framework default).
- [ ] "Made with AI" tag (or any equivalent attribution badge) is removed.
- [ ] Privacy Policy page exists at /privacy-policy with real, substantive content.
- [ ] Terms & Conditions page exists at /terms with real, substantive content.

---

## General Quality Standards

- Make no mistakes. Double-check logic, links, and content before marking any task done.
- Every design decision must have a clear purpose. No decoration for its own sake.
- Typography, spacing, and color must be consistent and intentional throughout.
- All interactive elements must be accessible (keyboard navigable, proper contrast ratios).
