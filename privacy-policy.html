Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Overview

DaddyDefender is a browser-based tool that analyzes URLs and text messages for phishing and fraud patterns. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the tool, and what we do with it.

The short version: analysis runs entirely inside your browser. The content you submit for analysis is never transmitted to any server we operate. We do not collect, store, or sell personal data.

What we collect

We collect no personal information from users of DaddyDefender. Specifically:

  • Submitted URLs and messages are processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. They are never sent to a server.
  • Account information does not exist. DaddyDefender has no user accounts, registration, or login.
  • Cookies are not used by DaddyDefender for tracking or analytics. If you visit this site via a hosting provider, that provider may set operational cookies; refer to their policy for details.

Analytics and third-party services

DaddyDefender does not load any third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics or similar). The only external resource loaded is Google Fonts for typography. Google Fonts may log the font request, which is subject to Google's own privacy policy. No personally identifiable information from your browsing session is shared through this request.

Data storage and retention

Because no data is transmitted to our servers, we retain nothing. Any content you type into DaddyDefender lives only in your browser's memory and is discarded when you close or refresh the tab. We have no access to it at any point.

Security

The client-side architecture of DaddyDefender is itself a security measure: your potentially sensitive URLs and messages never leave your device. Analysis is limited to pattern matching against documented threat indicators; no network call is required to produce a result.

That said, no software is without risk. You should not treat DaddyDefender's output as a definitive security clearance. Always exercise independent judgment before interacting with unfamiliar online content.

Children

DaddyDefender is a general-purpose security tool with no age restriction for use. Because we collect no personal data, no special handling of data from minors is required or performed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Because we hold no user data, there is no mechanism for us to notify you of changes directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project maintainer. Contact details are available through the official project repository or hosting platform associated with this site.
