    How the analysis works

    Multi-layered pipeline combining local structural heuristics, external threat intelligence lookups, and AI explanation assistance with local persistence.

    01

    Structural & heuristic inspection

    For URLs: checks hostnames, protocols, TLDs, subdomain depth, raw IP usage, hyphens, URL shorteners, and deceptive formatting characters.

    02

    Threat intelligence feeds

    Cross-references URLs against VirusTotal antivirus engines and URLhaus abuse databases to identify known active attack campaigns.

    03

    Scoring & AI explanation

    Integrates weighted indicators into a 0 to 100 risk score and generates concrete, actionable steps and clear plain-language summaries.

    DaddyDefender performs static pattern analysis and threat intelligence cross-checks. It does not guarantee detection of all zero-day threats. Always verify through official channels when in doubt.
